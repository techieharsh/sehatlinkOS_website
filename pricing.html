FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Pricing & Subscription FAQs

Clear answers to the most common billing and commercial questions from hospital administrators.

Are there any hidden costs or per-patient transaction fees? +

No. SehatLink OS operates on a transparent fixed subscription model. We do not charge any per-patient registration fee, per-prescription fee, or per-invoice cut. You can register unlimited patients within your active tier.

Do you charge extra for ABDM M1, M2, and M3 milestone integration? +

No. Complete ABDM integration—including 14-digit ABHA creation (M1), Health Information Provider FHIR linking (M2), and Health Information User consent gateway (M3)—is natively embedded into SehatLink OS at zero extra surcharge.

Can we migrate historical patient records from our old software? +

Yes! Our technical team provides complimentary data migration for patient demographics, historical visits, and pharmacy stock ledgers from Excel files, CSV spreadsheets, or SQL database dumps.

Can we upgrade or expand our plan as our bed capacity grows? +

Absolutely. SehatLink OS is fully modular. You can start with the Clinic Starter plan and upgrade to the Multi-Specialty Hospital or Enterprise edition seamlessly without losing any past data or configuration.

What payment modes are accepted for software subscriptions? +

We accept official institutional payments via NEFT / RTGS bank transfer, UPI QR code, Corporate Netbanking, and Debit / Credit Cards. All invoices are 100% GST-compliant with input tax credit eligibility.

How does the 14-day free product trial work? +

When you schedule a demo with our team, we set up a fully-functional sandbox instance of SehatLink OS pre-loaded with sample hospital data. Your doctors and staff can test-drive the actual system for 14 days with zero obligation.
