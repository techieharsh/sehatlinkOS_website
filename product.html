FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions About SehatLink OS & ABDM?

Here are answers to the most common queries from hospital administrators and IT managers.

Is SehatLink OS fully certified for ABDM M1, M2, and M3 milestones? +

Yes. SehatLink OS is built in strict accordance with the National Health Authority (NHA) standards, supporting ABHA number creation (M1), Health Information Provider record linking (M2), and Health Information User consent-based record retrieval (M3).

Can SehatLink OS be customized for single-doctor clinics vs 100+ bed hospitals? +

Absolutely. SehatLink OS is modular and scalable. Smaller clinics can enable only the OPD and Billing modules, while multi-specialty hospitals can activate IPD, Ayushman PM-JAY, ABDM M3, and multi-counter accounting.

How does technical support work during setup and live operations? +

DigiTech Innovations provides a dedicated 30+ member team of software engineers, implementation specialists, and 24/7 technical helpdesk support to assist with data migration, staff training, and live system maintenance.

Is patient health data secure on SehatLink OS? +

Yes. SehatLink OS utilizes end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption, FHIR cryptographic standards, strict role-based access control, and compliant database security measures to protect confidential clinical and financial records.
